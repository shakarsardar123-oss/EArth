Why this model is bundled
-------------------------
AURA's PRIMARY wake word ("Hey AURA") uses genuine on-device ACOUSTIC
keyword-spotting. The Vosk small English model is a Kaldi-based acoustic
model that runs fully offline. AURA loads it with a *grammar-constrained*
recognizer whose vocabulary is limited to the wake phrase plus an
out-of-vocabulary token:

    ["hey aura", "[unk]"]

Because decoding is constrained to that tiny grammar, the recognizer behaves
as an acoustic keyword spotter (mic PCM -> acoustic inference -> phrase
confidence), NOT a free-form speech-to-text transcript search. Word-level
confidences returned by the model gate every detection.
